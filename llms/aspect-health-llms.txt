# Aspect Health

> Aspect Health is a digital health company offering a science-backed program for women managing PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome), combining continuous glucose monitoring (CGM), coaching from board-certified health coaches, at-home lab testing, and the Aida AI dietitian, delivered through a mobile app. It is a consumer (B2C) platform and does not currently publish a public developer API or SDK.

## Programs
- [PCOS Program](https://aspect-health.com/): CGM monitoring, personalized coaching, and at-home lab testing
- [Weight Loss Program](https://aspect-health.com/weight-purchase): Weight management program
- [Women's Health Program](https://aspect-health.com/womens-health): General women's health program
- [21 Day Check Up](https://aspect-health.com/checkup): Assessment service
- [Coaches](https://aspect-health.com/coaching): Board-certified health coaches
- [Science](https://aspect-health.com/aspect-science): The research behind the program
